## 1. Identity, positioning & mission

- **The post-trade engine:** OSTTRA built the infrastructure the world's post-trade financial system runs on: a trusted network that has steadied markets through decades of change and volatility. We power the post-trade ecosystem that keeps markets moving.
- **Core positioning statement:**
  > *"OSTTRA brings together the world’s trading community to solve its post-trade challenges, engineering and operating the systems and standards that keep markets moving. Always on, always optimising, our networks unite counterparties, workflows and data into one collaborative ecosystem that creates efficiency, unlocks capital and enables financial markets to flow seamlessly."*
- **Corporate narrative statement:** OSTTRA is the architect of the post-trade financial system. Its network capture, processing, and lifecycle management are designed to steady markets through volatility, simplify industry complexity, translate regulatory changes, and release capital. The ambition is to build an open, intelligent, and co-created ecosystem that transforms shared infrastructure into shared benefit.
- **Heritage & footprint:** Built on 25 years of proven market resilience, OSTTRA operates the core infrastructure and collaborative networks that keep financial markets moving.

## 2. The three narrative pillars (corporate strategy reference)

*(Synthesised from the Lansons Corporate Brand Playbook for high-level corporate positioning.)*

### Pillar 1: The foundation the market runs on
- **Core narrative claims & defining statements:**
  - *"The infrastructure the world’s post-trade financial system runs on."*
  - *"A trusted network and independent custodian of sensitive trade data."*
  - *"Steadied markets through decades of change and volatility."*
  - *"Every day, millions of transactions flow through our network."*
- **Grounding evidence:**
  - Critical market infrastructure processing ~80 million trades every month (~1 billion annually).
  - Network of 9,000+ counterparties (spanning banks, investment managers, trading venues, and CCPs).
  - Relied on by 30 Global Systemically Important Banks (GSIBs).
  - Processes >90% of global interest rate derivatives and reconciles >90% of all bilateral OTC derivatives globally.
  - "Single source of agreement" through electronic legal confirmation, providing a standardised, authoritative record for downstream post-trade workflows.
  - Proven crisis resilience across periods of extreme market stress and regulatory upheaval from the 2008 Lehman Brothers collapse to modern structural reforms.

### Pillar 2: The network that adapts and leads
- **Core narrative claims & defining statements:**
  - *"We are always on, always optimising."*
  - *"We translate industry change into workable process."*
  - *"We simplify complexity and collaborate with our network to solve post-trade challenges."*
- **Grounding evidence:**
  - Runs ~40 multilateral optimisation cycles every week; compressed >$250 trillion in gross notional over the preceding 12 months.
  - Disciplined technology investment allocating 70% of spend to core platform resilience, 20% to innovation, and 10% to platform modernisation.
  - Active regulatory translator engaging directly with policymakers to turn mandates into operational workflows ahead of deadlines (20+ years delivering clearing mandates, reporting, EMIR, Dodd-Frank, SFTR, and the IBOR to RFR benchmark transition).
  - Manages ~$300 billion in margin calls monthly, driving systematic capital and exposure reduction.
  - AI-enabled dispute explanation directing operations teams to prioritise risky breaks over routine noise.
  - Convenes 1,600+ active market participants in collaborative working groups and advisory forums to co-develop solutions.

### Pillar 3: Building the future of post-trade together
- **Core narrative claims & defining statements:**
  - *"OSTTRA is engineering the next generation of post-trade."*
  - *"Our ambition is to connect data, workflows and standards."*
  - *"Together we will transform financial markets."*
- **Grounding evidence:**
  - Active innovation pipeline extending core infrastructure into scalable, modern workflows.
  - Applied innovation: deploying machine learning, analytics, and workflow automation directly into reconciliation, dispute management, and collateral operations.
  - Leveraging multi-business datasets to develop advanced analytics for exposure monitoring and risk management.
  - Network effects: each participant among the 9,000+ connected counterparties strengthens a neutral, shared market infrastructure.

## 3. Five core brand actions

All copy and capability statements must align with our five core brand actions, driven by strong, active verbs:

1. **Connect:** We unite thousands of counterparties globally, linking trusted infrastructure with next-generation technologies.
2. **Process:** We simplify complexity and automate workflows across the entire trade lifecycle (from trade capture to settlement).
3. **Mitigate:** We proactively reduce systemic, operational, and bilateral settlement risks.
4. **Reconcile:** We standardise agreements to make reconciliation and margin practices highly accurate, directly leading to fewer disputes and greater operational resilience.
5. **Optimise:** We deliver continuous, automated optimisation of margin, collateral, capital, and liquidity.

---

## 4. Tone & voice rules

- **Confident, authoritative, and direct:** Speak as industry veterans and market leaders. We do not "try" or "hope" to help. Use active framing:
  - *"We enable"*
  - *"We power"*
  - *"We simplify"*
  - *"We translate"*
- **No fluff or salesy jargon:** Avoid emotional, speculative, or hyper-promotional language. Focus on concrete operational metrics, risk reduction, and replacing fragile manual workflows with robust automation.
- **Clear and accessible:** Translate industry complexity and regulatory shifts into straightforward, structured, workable operational processes.

---

## 5. Ironclad brand capitalisation: ALWAYS "OSTTRA"

- **Rule:** The brand name must **ALWAYS** be written in full capital letters: **OSTTRA**.
- **Prohibited forms:**
  - **NEVER** write `Osttra` (title case is an automatic critical lint failure).
  - **NEVER** write `osttra` (lowercase is an automatic critical lint failure).
- **Scope:** This applies across all headings, body text, tables, decks, diagrams, capability cards, and code documentation.
- *(Note: Technical internet standards for email addresses like `name@osttra.com` and web domain URLs like `osttra.com` naturally use lowercase per internet RFCs; all references in written prose or display titles must strictly be **OSTTRA**).*

## 6. The seven post-trade solution families & renaming architecture

Per OSTTRA's official enterprise solution renaming (`https://osttra.com/post-trade-solutions-renaming/`), our products have transitioned into **seven unified solution families**.

All material intended for wide internal distribution or external client-facing collateral must use the new solution family nomenclature. (For grammatical mechanics, UK spelling tables, and heading conventions, see `house-style.md`).

### The seven unified solution families

1. **OSTTRA Connect:**
   - *Core Capability:* Trade capture, matching, confirmation, allocation, give-up, clearing, and regulatory reporting.
   - *Description:* Brings together a unique network of counterparties and industry utilities to automate the workflows required to confirm, give-up, allocate, clear, and report trades.
2. **OSTTRA Resolve:**
   - *Core Capability:* Portfolio and position reconciliation.
   - *Description:* Aligns positions and reduces risk by reconciling portfolios and managing exceptions across a network of 2,000+ global counterparties. Replaces legacy *triResolve*.
3. **OSTTRA Margin:**
   - *Core Capability:* Complete collateral management.
   - *Description:* Streamlines the collateral management process with automated margin workflows and dispute resolution tools. Replaces legacy *triResolve Margin*.
4. **OSTTRA Calculate:**
   - *Core Capability:* Margin and risk calculations.
   - *Description:* Delivers margin and risk calculations, XVA analytics, valuations, and exposure profiles to support trading decisions and post-trade operations. Replaces legacy *triCalculate*.
5. **OSTTRA Reduce:**
   - *Core Capability:* Multilateral portfolio compression.
   - *Description:* Cuts operational risk and capital costs by reducing gross notional exposures and line items for cleared and non-cleared OTC derivative portfolios. Replaces legacy *triReduce*.
6. **OSTTRA Rebalance:**
   - *Core Capability:* Counterparty risk optimisation.
   - *Description:* Optimises counterparty risk exposures and reduces the cost of funding initial margin (IM) and capital requirements through multilateral portfolio optimisation. Replaces legacy *triBalance*.
7. **OSTTRA Reset:**
   - *Core Capability:* Fixing and strike risk management.
   - *Description:* Reduces second-order fixing and strike risks that build up in derivative portfolios over time, using an advanced matching engine to generate offsetting trades. Replaces legacy *Reset*.

### Operational boundaries of the renaming
- **What changes:**
  - System names displayed on login portals, UI application screens, and user guides.
  - Client-facing and market documentation, factsheets, roadmaps, and presentation decks.
  - Client support email addresses and ticketing channels.
  - Client invoices and commercial service schedules.
- **What does NOT change:**
  - Technical APIs, SFTP protocols, and inbound/outbound payload message formats.
  - Short-term web URLs and endpoint hostnames.
  - Existing legal contracts and master service agreements (MSAs remain fully binding and legally valid).
